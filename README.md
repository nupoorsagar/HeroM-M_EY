# HeroM-M_EY
Challenge III: Automotive [Hero + M&M]  
 The automotive sector currently utilizes mainly a reactive maintenance model, which only takes place after a failure has occurred or when a set mileage threshold has been reached. Despite the fact that vehicles have progressively transitioned to very sophisticated computer-driven machines capable of generating huge amounts of telemetry data, maintenance ecosystems remains heavily analog and disparate. 

The "break-fix" model results in great deals of inefficiency, as well as points of friction between the three main stakeholders: the vehicle owner, the service provider, and the original equipment manufacturer (OEM). 

The Challenge: A Fragmented, Reactive System  

For Customers - Unanticipated breakdowns create long periods of unacceptable downtime, escalating anxiety, and eroding brand trust.  
For Service Centers- Reactive "feast or famine" work backlogs and underutilization lead to severe operational inefficiency.  
For Manufacturers- Slow, disconnected "feedback loops" from the field result in missed or recurring defects, leading to disastrous mass recalls.  

Why is there a need to address this problem??????

1.  Data vs. Utilization - The vehicle sensor data already exists; it just isn't being used.  
2. The Growing Presence of Agentic AI- We now have the ability to not only analyze data, but also autonomously act on it.  
3.  Unique Competitive Differentiation - The experience you provide to the customer when they own the vehicle is the brand differentiator.

Nature of Output: Web-based dashboard for service centers & OEM teamsm , Voice chatbot + web/mobile interface for vehicle owners, Real-time alerts, scheduling, diagnostics, and quality insights

#Service Center Dashboard:

Real-time appointment calendar with color-coded priority levels (Critical/High/Medium/Low)
Bay utilization metrics and technician assignment
AI prediction alerts for upcoming maintenance needs
Customer 360° view with pre-populated diagnostic reports
Parts inventory management with AI-powered reordering
Performance analytics showing efficiency improvements with AI

#OEM Quality Dashboard:

Executive KPIs: Fleet health score, active vehicles, recall risk index, warranty costs, AI accuracy
Critical quality alerts with batch-level defect detection (e.g., Battery Batch #BT-2019-Q3)
Top failure components with trend analysis
Supplier quality scorecards with performance ratings
Predictive insights showing emerging patterns and cost optimization opportunities
Early warning system detecting issues 14 days vs traditional 120 days
